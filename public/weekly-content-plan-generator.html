FAQ

How many times a week should a business post?Three to five quality posts a week is a realistic target for most small teams. Consistency matters more than volume.

What are content pillars?Recurring themes you rotate through, so your feed isn't all selling. Five common pillars are educate, inspire, entertain, connect and promote.

How much of my content should be promotional?Roughly one post in five. The other posts earn the attention that makes the promotional ones work.
